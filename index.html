Remedial

Here are 10 ways to save money in high school

 

Open a savings account. A savings account gives you amazing access to your funds.

Apply for a summer job. Try to find a part time job over summer. It is a fantastic experience and is a better way to earn money than doing chores for your parents.

Buy used textbooks/sell the text books. Buy much more cheaper textbooks from older friends who finished high-school a year or two before you.

Do not impulse buy, write it down and wait a week. Do not buy things that you do not need.

Never go grocery shopping when you are hungry. This is a classic bit of advice but it is pretty simple. Take a shopping list.

Have a go at Gumtree. You can have extra money by selling things that you no longer used

Google Flight. This is to manage your schedule so that you can be notiied immediately.

Bring food from home. Bringing food from home is cheaper and it can be really good once you have found a few lunches that you like.
